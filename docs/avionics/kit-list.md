# Kit list

What a complete avionics kit contains. This is the reference copy: use it when you check your kit at issue, when you pack it up at the end of the unit, and whenever you're trying to work out what something is.

Kits are numbered, and there are 12 of them. Everything in the list is signed out to your group and comes back at the end of the unit in reusable order.

| Box | Item | Qty | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cube | Cube Orange+ on ADS-B carrier board | 1 | Orange Cube in a black carrier board |
| Cube | USB/buzzer cable | 1 | USB extender with buzzer cable |
| Power | Power Brick Mini | 1 | Small power regulator with a yellow connector at each end |
| Power | POWER2 cable | 1 | Molex CLIK-Mate 2 mm to 6-pin JST-GH, labelled "POWER2" |
| Power | UBEC | 1 | Orange Matek Systems UBEC with 2 red/black wire sets |
| Telem | Kahuna ESP | 1 | Purple box with antenna |
| Telem | JST telemetry cable | 1 | 6-pin JST-GH to 6-pin JST-GH, labelled "GH TELEM CABLE" |
| ADC | I²C ADC | 1 | PCB labelled "16-Bit ADC Module" |
| ADC | ADC cable | 1 | Red/black/blue/green cable |
| USB | USB cable | 1 | Green USB-C to micro-USB cable |
| USB | USB adapter | 1 | Black female USB-C to male USB-A cable |
| Misc. | Shaft-mount potentiometer | 2 | Small white components mounted on pink foam |

!!! tip "Something missing or damaged?"

    Tell the unit team as soon as you notice, rather than at the end of the year. Replacements can have long lead times.
