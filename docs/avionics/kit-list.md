# Kit list

What a complete avionics kit contains. This is the reference copy: use it when you check your kit at issue, when you pack it up at the end of the unit, and whenever you're trying to work out what something is.

Kits are numbered, and there are 12 of them. Everything in the list is signed out to your group and comes back at the end of the unit in reusable order.

!!! tip "Missing or damaged?"

    Tell the unit team as soon as you notice, rather than at the end of the unit. Replacements can have long lead times, and a kit that's short of a cable stops being a working system.
