## What this session covers

**Part 1 — Avionics**

- Who's who, and where things happen
- What avionics does in your wind-tunnel model
- What the requirements specification asks for, and what "done" looks like this teaching block
- Integration: the interfaces between avionics and every other division
- The workshop programme, and how to get ready

**Part 2 — Mechanisms**

- What has to move: flap, aileron, elevator, rudder, leading edge device, landing gear
- The design task: kinematics in the Linkage app, then bearings, backlash and buildability
- Where mechanisms meets avionics — the servo cable, and who owns which side of it
- What broke last year, and the mechanisms sessions from week 3
- Design realisation: practical geometry, transferring torque and motion, pivots and joints, standard parts, machining and metrology, tolerancing
